# Intro
Most people make coffee the same way every morning, and most of them are leaving flavour on the table. In the next couple of minutes, I'll show you three simple changes that make a bigger difference than any expensive machine.

# Fresh beans, ground just before brewing
Coffee starts losing its aroma within minutes of being ground. So buy whole beans, look for a roast date in the last month, and grind only what you need, right before you brew. [pause 0.5] If you change just one thing, make it this.

# Get the ratio right
The most common mistake is simply using too little coffee. A good starting point is one gram of coffee for every sixteen grams of water. That's about sixty grams per litre. Use a kitchen scale, not a scoop, and you'll get the same great cup every single day.

# Mind the water temperature
Boiling water scorches the grounds and pulls out bitter flavours. Aim for somewhere between ninety and ninety-six degrees Celsius. No thermometer? Just let the kettle rest for about thirty seconds after it boils.
